Validate Task and Grader Invariants

By the end of this hour

  • Audit the entire frozen private suite without model calls.
  • Verify that manifests identify the exact measurement artifacts.

Before you begin

  • The private freeze manifest from the previous session.
  • Reference, no-op, and broken controls for all 24 planned tasks.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

This audit closes the gap between a manifest that looks complete and a suite that can actually be recreated. Rebuild fixtures from the frozen revision and run control checks in clean directories. Compare the resulting hashes and split counts with the manifest rather than relying on filenames or memory. Development controls may be rerun freely; final fixtures may be validated without running agents. The audit produces a trustworthy instrument for the later study and a precise record of what was frozen when.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    Predict the suite counts: total tasks, development tasks, and final tasks. Explain which runs are allowed today.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Spend 5 minutes on H17: Parametrized suite-level invariant checks. Spend 5 minutes on H24: Compare rebuilt fixture identities. Stop at the time limit and use the page as a reference while building.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Implement a validator that rebuilds all 24 frozen fixtures in fresh directories and checks unique IDs,8/16 split membership, valid paths, recorded hashes, and reference/no-op/broken control outcomes. Add negative test inputs with a duplicate ID, wrong split count, modified fixture, and missing grader; require explicit failures. Run the validator on the real frozen manifest. Repair mechanical defects before any final-agent run by writing a new version and preserving a change note. Keep the command model-free.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Save evals/fixture-audit.json or an equivalent table with counts, hashes, control outcomes, and the authoritative frozen revision.

What to save

Passing manifest validation and four negative regression tests.

Watch for: Keep the audit command free of model calls; this is still benchmark validation, not final evaluation.

Check your understanding

Is “task-07” sufficient information to reproduce a result?

Check your reasoning

No. The task ID also needs its fixture, grader, dependencies, and revisions; otherwise the same name may refer to a different measurement.
