Group Private Suite Failures And Costs

By the end of this hour

  • Summarize task outcomes without losing failed attempts.
  • Compare time, tokens, and cost by task and condition.

Before you begin

  • The reconciled per-trial outcome table and raw usage records.
  • The predeclared metric definitions and trial accounting rules.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

A result table should preserve the cost of failure. Summarizing only successful runs can make a policy appear cheap and fast by excluding the difficult cases that consumed its budget. Group trials by task and condition first, then derive the declared success and resource summaries from all attempts. Keep terminal outcome and diagnostic failure category separate: several failure mechanisms can produce the same unsuccessful task result. Use the frozen metric definitions so this initial analysis does not become a search for whichever summary favors one policy.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    Explain how excluding timeouts can bias both success rate and latency or cost summaries.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Spend 5 minutes on H25: Means, medians, quantiles, and input assumptions. Spend 5 minutes on H4: State success denominators explicitly. Stop at the time limit and use the page as a reference while building.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Load the authoritative trial table and assert its IDs match the manifest. Group by task, condition, and failure category. Produce per-trial success counts, per-task mean success across three repeats, total and per-task cost, tokens, and declared time summaries. Include capped and truncated attempts as failures under the study rule while retaining their actual recorded resource usage. Record missing billing fields explicitly rather than replacing them with zero. Save machine-readable tables plus one small plot or readable comparison table that includes denominators and units. Compute three-repeat per-task means only where all planned repeats were attempted. Preserve attempted/planned counts and apply the predeclared incomplete-study rule: do not silently divide by three, impute unattempted rows as failures, or drop them. Label an incomplete study descriptive.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Check aggregate totals against raw trial counts and usage. Save the analysis command and required private-suite tables without changing the frozen harness.

What to save

Private-suite outcome and cost tables.

Watch for: Do not silently coerce unknown usage into zero cost.

Check your understanding

Can a policy with lower successful-run cost still have higher overall cost?

Check your reasoning

Yes. It may spend more on failed attempts or retries. Report resource use across all attempts as well as any explicitly labeled successful-run subset.
