Freeze Metrics Splits And Cost Estimates

By the end of this hour

  • Predeclare metrics and task-grouped uncertainty.

Before you begin

  • The matched-condition specification from the previous session.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

Three trials on one task are repeated observations of the same task, not three independent task problems. Keep that grouping when planning summaries and uncertainty. The main study contains sixteen final tasks, two conditions, and three repeats: ninety-six attempts. Report all attempts, including deadline and budget failures. Cost planning should use development usage plus a conservative cap-based upper bound, including retries and compaction. If the plan is unaffordable, reduce limits consistently or defer evaluation before seeing final outcomes; do not discard expensive failures afterward.

Check your understanding

Why is reporting “any of three succeeded” as single-try success misleading?

Check your reasoning

It gives each task multiple chances while labeling the outcome as one chance. Report per-trial success separately and label repeated-trial summaries accurately.
