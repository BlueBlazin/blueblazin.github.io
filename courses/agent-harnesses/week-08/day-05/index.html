Launch The First Matched Trial Round

By the end of this hour

  • Launch the first thirty-two final trials under frozen conditions.
  • Capture outcomes and usage without changing the evaluated harness.

Before you begin

  • The validated 96-trial manifest.
  • A working background runner, trace storage, and total-study budget stop rule.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

Once final evaluation begins, observation is for operational integrity rather than tuning. You may check that traces are being written and limits are enforced, but do not adjust prompts or policies after seeing a difficult final task. Background execution can outlast this study hour; your active work is to launch, inspect basic health, and leave a bounded job. Each attempt needs a terminal outcome or an explicit running/interrupted status. An unsuccessful trial is evidence and must remain in the manifest-derived accounting.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    Name the difference between an operational check and final-set-driven tuning. Give one example of each.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Spend 5 minutes on H9: Request IDs, timeout errors, and usage records. Spend 5 minutes on H4: Retain every attempted outcome. Stop at the time limit and use the page as a reference while building.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Launch repeat one for all sixteen tasks and both conditions using the frozen manifest and declared concurrency. Confirm fresh workspaces, per-trial time/cost caps, unique trace paths, and usage recording on the first started pair. Monitor aggregate job status and cumulative spending without changing prompts, tools, or context policy. If an infrastructure fault occurs, record it under the predeclared rule and preserve attempted runs. Leave any long-running bounded jobs in the background; do not extend the scheduled hour merely to watch them finish.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Save a first-round status table covering all 32 scheduled trials, with attempted/running/terminal states, trace locations, and known charges. Reconcile final outcomes when the jobs finish.

What to save

First 32 attempted trials are recorded.

Watch for: Do not leave an unbounded cloud or API loop running after the study hour.

Check your understanding

Should a timeout disappear from the result table when a later rerun succeeds?

Check your reasoning

No. The original attempt remains a failure under its budget. Any permitted replacement or additional run must be explicitly labeled and accounted for.
