Implement The Functional Training Step

By the end of this hour

  • Implement a jitted training step with explicit optimizer state.
  • Match loss reduction and AdamW semantics across frameworks.

Before you begin

  • Full-model forward parity on CPU FP32.
  • The verified PyTorch AdamW training step and shared token batch.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

A functional training step maps (parameters, optimizer state, batch, RNG) to updated state and metrics. Differentiation produces a gradient pytree with the same parameter structure; the optimizer transforms those gradients using explicit moments and counters. Matching an optimizer name is insufficient for cross-framework parity: epsilon placement, bias correction, decay masks, clipping order and step indexing must agree. Compare a single update before relying on a long loss curve. JIT can then wrap the pure step while logging remains outside as returned scalar metrics.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    Write the complete input/output signature of a pure training step, including the optimizer counter and random key.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Consult A4’s Training Cookbook for explicit training state. Compare your intended optimizer settings with the PyTorch scalar-update note.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Implement or use a small explicit-state AdamW implementation with documented equations and matching parameter-decay policy. Define the shifted-token mean loss, calculate value and gradients, apply the same global-norm clipping rule as PyTorch, and return new parameters, optimizer state, RNG and metrics. Test one unjitted update from the shared initial state before adding jit. Then call the jitted step several times on the fixed batch, passing its returned state into the next call. Keep metric conversion and file logging outside the compiled function.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Save optimizer settings and a one-step comparison of loss, selected updated weights and moment values. Confirm the optimizer counter advances once per update.

What to save

Functional training state updates correctly without hidden mutation.

Watch for: Do not repeatedly pass the initial optimizer state into the jitted step; that resets moment accumulation every iteration.

Check your understanding

Why might two AdamW implementations diverge immediately even with equal gradients?

Check your reasoning

They can differ in epsilon placement, decay exclusions/order, bias correction, step counters or clipping. Equal names and learning rates alone do not establish equal update equations.
