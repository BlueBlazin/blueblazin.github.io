Interpret The GQA Versus MHA Study

By the end of this hour

  • Interpret cache savings and latency measurements together.
  • State the study’s limits without claiming quality equivalence.

Before you begin

  • The correctness/storage CSV, raw timing samples and frozen protocol.
  • Parameter counts for both attention configurations.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

A good performance report separates deterministic accounting from empirical timing. Cache elements can be derived from dimensions; elapsed time depends on the implementation and measurement conditions. A reduced cache may help some decode workloads while leaving others dominated by launch overhead or computation. Show negative or inconclusive results as clearly as wins. Because this study changes K/V parameterization and does not train quality-matched models, its evidence supports statements about these implementations and workloads, not claims that the two architectures are equally capable.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    Predict the theoretical cache ratio from the two KV-head counts before opening the measured table.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Review A3 only for the structural grouping claim used in the report; keep your measured results distinct from the paper’s training results.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Write experiments/gqa-vs-mha/report.md with a configuration table, correctness summary, cache-byte table and separate prefill/decode timing tables. Report median and spread with sample count. Explain at least one result using a plausible workload mechanism, labeling it a hypothesis unless you profiled it. Include the changed K/V parameter counts and two limitations: the measured shape/device range and the absence of quality-matched training. Link raw data and the exact benchmark command. If no reliable latency difference appears, report that outcome rather than widening the experiment until a win appears.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Recalculate one reported ratio from raw values. Check every speed claim names the workload and device, and that no sentence claims quality equivalence.

What to save

Matched study reports correctness, memory, prefill, decode and qualified conclusions.

Watch for: A ratio from one prompt length should not be advertised as a universal decoder speedup.

Check your understanding

What conclusion remains valid if GQA uses less cache but has the same measured latency in your tiny fixture?

Check your reasoning

The measured implementation reduces retained cache under the tested assumptions; a latency benefit was not established for that workload.
