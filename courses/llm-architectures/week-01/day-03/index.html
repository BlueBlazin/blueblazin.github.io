Assemble Your First GPT Block

By the end of this hour

  • Assemble a pre-norm GPT-style residual block.
  • Verify causal behavior after combining all components.

Before you begin

  • The tested attention module from the previous session.
  • Stable loss and fixed token IDs.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

A pre-norm decoder block computes:

u=x+Attention⁡(LayerNorm⁡(x)),y=u+MLP⁡(LayerNorm⁡(u)).\begin{aligned} u &= x+\operatorname{Attention}(\operatorname{LayerNorm}(x)),\\ y &= u+\operatorname{MLP}(\operatorname{LayerNorm}(u)). \end{aligned}uy​=x+Attention(LayerNorm(x)),=u+MLP(LayerNorm(u)).​

The MLP expands the channel width, applies GELU and projects back. Residual additions require equal outer shapes, but they do not guarantee meaningful gradients or causality. Learned position embeddings belong at the model input: token and position vectors are added before the first block, not repeatedly inside every block. This session builds a minimal composition whose behavior you can inspect before stacking layers and training it.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    Write the two residual equations from memory and identify which tensors must have matching shape.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Use A1 to compare normalization placement, residual paths and the feedforward expansion. Trace one token through the block.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Add LayerNorm, a C→4C→C GELU feedforward module, and the two pre-norm residual additions around your attention and MLP. Separately create a small token-embedding and learned-position-embedding input wrapper, checking that sequence length stays within the position table. Feed the fixed token sample through one block and a temporary linear vocabulary head. Backpropagate the stable loss and inspect finite gradients. Repeat the future-token perturbation test on the composed path; keep dropout disabled while establishing correctness.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Save a block diagram or shape table and rerun loss, attention and composition tests. Record parameter groups and one finite gradient norm without starting a training experiment.

What to save

GPT block passes shape, causality and stable-loss checks.

Watch for: Do not add learned positions inside every repeated block when aiming for this GPT-style model.

Check your understanding

Why does a position embedding not violate causality?

Check your reasoning

It supplies the token’s own position index, not the content of future tokens. Causality is preserved as long as all mixing across sequence positions obeys the causal mask.
