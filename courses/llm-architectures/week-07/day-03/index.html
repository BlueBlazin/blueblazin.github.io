Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

Architecture families evolve, and a familiar acronym is not evidence that a newer design uses exactly the same mechanism. Start from the current report’s definitions, then compare its retained state and attention-selection rules with your previous MLA and GQA notes. Distinguish a reported design choice from a reported benchmark result: the former can be traced structurally, while the latter depends on the authors’ full training and evaluation setup. This session extends your component map without introducing another required implementation or a model download.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    From memory, state the difference between compressing cached content and choosing which past positions to attend to.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Read A11’s relevant architecture descriptions for compressed or sparse attention, routing, position and training choices. Follow definitions before drawing analogies.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Fill the DeepSeek-V4 column in notes/current-architectures.md using the same rows as yesterday. For attention/cache, explicitly record what state is retained and whether the description also changes how prior positions are selected. Add expert-routing and positional details only where supported by the report. Record one documented training choice without turning it into a requirement for your project. Place two short comparison notes below the table: one relationship to your MLA trace and one concrete difference from your dense GQA decoder. Keep missing details visible and all benchmark claims attributed.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Check each added cell against its linked passage and ensure no claim was inherited merely from DeepSeek-V2 or V3. Save the expanded table.
