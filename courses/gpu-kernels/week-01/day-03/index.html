Profile and Explain Dominant Operations

By the end of this hour

  • Identify three measured decoder costs from a short profile.
  • Compare FLOP and byte estimates with the earlier bottleneck predictions.

Before you begin

  • Your timing utility and recorded GPU environment.
  • The three predictions from 1-1 and a fixed decoder input.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

A profile connects source operations with device activity, but interpretation requires care. Inclusive time can contain child operations; adding parent and child times double-counts work. A matrix product with dimensions M by K and K by N performs approximately 2MKN floating-point operations. Reading two inputs and writing an output gives a lower-bound traffic estimate, not a complete account of cache behavior or intermediate tensors. Arithmetic intensity is operations divided by bytes moved. Use these estimates to explain observations, while keeping measured timings distinct from assumptions about the underlying bottleneck.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    Recall your three predictions. For C = AB, state the output shape and approximate FLOP count without consulting notes.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Use K1 profiling material and K2 arithmetic-intensity discussion to distinguish measured time from inferred bandwidth or compute limits.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Capture a short warmed decoder profile on the same fixed batch; keep the trace window to a few iterations so inspection fits this hour. Group operations consistently and select the three largest relevant self-device costs, avoiding nested double counting. For each, record shapes, dtype, call count, time, approximate FLOPs, and a labeled lower-bound byte estimate. Compare the ranking with your predictions and identify one surprise. Save the trace and a three-row table in reports/kernels/decoder-profile.md. If you also profile MPS, put it in a separately labeled table.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Check that your table units are explicit, operation counts match tensor dimensions, and inferred bottlenecks are described as hypotheses. Save the trace command and stop the GPU.

What to save

Three-operation bottleneck table with measurements, estimates, and separate MPS results if used.

Watch for: Do not sum inclusive parent time together with its child kernel times.

Check your understanding

Does a low arithmetic-intensity estimate prove an operation is bandwidth-bound?

Check your reasoning

No. It supports a hypothesis, but launch overhead, small problem size, poor access patterns, or limited parallelism may dominate.
