Study Modern Attention Hardware Tradeoffs

By the end of this hour

  • Compare modern attention scheduling with your restricted implementation.
  • Distinguish algorithmic ideas from hardware-specific claims.

Before you begin

  • Your restricted Triton attention forward and its support contract.
  • K7 FA4 paper/article and CuTe DSL example.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

A modern attention implementation combines the same mathematical operation with hardware-aware tiling, memory movement, synchronization, and scheduling choices. Those choices may target a different accelerator generation or precision path from your restricted teaching kernel. A useful comparison identifies an actual code path or measured bottleneck instead of treating a paper's headline speedup as universal. Your implementation intentionally has a small contract, so the comparison should explain what extra capability or scheduling sophistication buys and costs. Reading a newer kernel does not require porting all of its optimizations into the course project.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    List the hardware bottlenecks and omitted capabilities of your restricted attention forward.

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Read selected K7 hardware/scheduling discussion and one corresponding CuTe example; confirm the hardware targeted by each claim.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Write a three-row comparison covering data movement, computation scheduling, and supported problem scope. For each row, describe your implementation, one concrete K7 design choice, and the hardware or workload condition that makes the difference relevant. Link the exact paper passage or source location rather than relying on a headline. Include one optimization that cannot be assumed to transfer to your available GPU. Finish with a small future experiment that could test a single transferable idea, clearly labeling it unperformed. No new framework installation or benchmark campaign is required today.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Save the comparison with hardware/version qualifiers and a clear boundary between observed course results and reported external results.

What to save

Short comparison tied to code paths and hardware bottlenecks.

Watch for: Calling a newer attention algorithm universally faster without matching its tested hardware and workload.

Check your understanding

Why is a speedup reported on another accelerator insufficient evidence for your kernel report?

Check your reasoning

The hardware, baseline, shapes, precision, and supported features may differ, so the ratio does not establish performance under your conditions.
