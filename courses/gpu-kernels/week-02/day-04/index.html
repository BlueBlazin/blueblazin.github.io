Benchmark Softmax Against Both Baselines

By the end of this hour

  • Benchmark softmax against eager composition and compiled composition.
  • Interpret fused traffic and retain both speedups and regressions.

Before you begin

  • Passing Triton softmax with its support matrix.
  • Your timing utility and a pinned torch.compile-capable environment.

Environment or workflow question? Day 0 handbook.

The key idea

Fusion can eliminate repeated global-memory passes through intermediate arrays, but it is not automatically faster at every shape. A one-program-per-row design may expose too little parallelism when there are few rows, or require too many resources for wide rows. The eager baseline for this exercise is the explicit stable sequence of maximum, subtraction, exponential, sum, and division. Compile that same function to create the second baseline. A library softmax is a useful additional reference, but should not be mislabeled as an unfused composition. Compare equivalent input precision, output dtype, and allocation boundaries.

Your 60-minute plan

  1. Retrieve

    5 min

    List the intermediate tensors written by your eager stable-softmax expression. Which can remain on chip in the fused version?

  2. Study

    10 min

    Use the core resources above for ten minutes in total, focusing only on the indicated sections. Use K3’s fused-softmax performance discussion to frame hypotheses about row count and width, without assuming the tutorial’s speedups apply to your GPU.

  3. Build

    35 min

    Select six supported shapes covering few/many rows and narrow/wide rows. Implement an explicit stable eager function and compile the same function; validate both outputs against the saved reference. Warm compilation and your Triton kernel before timing. Measure each candidate sequentially with resident inputs and at least three independent batches. Save raw samples, median, spread, and separately timed cold compilation. Add the ideal 2MN-element payload estimate and label it as a lower-bound model. Explain one win or loss using row parallelism or intermediate traffic.

  4. Verify and save

    10 min

    Check matching dtype, shapes, and allocation semantics, then save reports/kernels/softmax.md. Keep every selected shape in the table, including unfavorable results.

What to save

Correctness matrix and fair timing table with bandwidth interpretation.

Watch for: Do not call torch.softmax an unfused multi-operation baseline without inspecting what that operator actually executes.

Check your understanding

Why might a fused kernel lose when there is only one short row?

Check your reasoning

It has little work and little parallelism; launch and wrapper overhead can dominate any reduction in intermediate traffic.
