# Specimen analogies

One illustration per entry, in the engraved, hand-tinted style of the seed set.

## I · The Seed: the literal kernel

- **Atomic core**: a sectioned mineral sphere like an onion-skin agate: a dense red centre wrapped in two thin shells, with a few loose beads orbiting far outside
- **Ignition kernel**: a sectioned seed pod bursting open at its heart, a small ochre spark-like core with fine radiating filaments
- **Corn / grain**: a single wheat grain cut lengthwise, showing bran, starchy endosperm and the small germ
- **TRISO fuel**: a tiny coated particle in cross-section, many concentric dark and grey layers around a small solid red kernel
- **Nut kernel**: a walnut split in half, the convoluted edible kernel inside the woody shell

## II · The Core: the trusted center

- **Linux / OS**: an antique anatomical heart with its great vessels branching out to every side: the central organ through which everything flows and which supplies every part of the body
- **Lean**: an assayer's touchstone: a flat black slate with a bright gold streak rubbed across it, a small gold nugget beside it, and two thin reference needles of gold laid next to the streak for comparison
- **Jupyter**: an antique brass pinwheel calculating machine with its crank handle and a row of number wheels
- **Microkernel**: a watchmaker's tray: at its centre only the escapement and balance wheel on a small bridge, and the other wheels and parts of the movement laid out separately around it
- **CGAL geometry**: an open case of brass drawing instruments: compass, ruling pen, protractor and set square in their fitted slots
- **CAD modeling**: a set of carved boxwood geometric solids: a cylinder, a block with rounded edges, and a sphere cut cleanly by a plane
- **Exokernel**: a bare watch movement with no case and no dial at all, its plates and wheels fully exposed
- **LCF / HOL**: a small locked iron strongbox with a single wax seal over its keyhole
- **Security kernel**: an antique lever padlock drawn in cut-away, showing the levers and bolt that check the key
- **Simulation engine**: the pinned brass cylinder of a music box beside its steel comb, each pin a scheduled note
- **Wolfram kernel**: a column of brass digit wheels from a difference engine

## III · The Routine: one function, run in parallel

- **CUDA**: a wooden frame of honeycomb seen face-on, with one worker bee busy in every cell, all doing the same task at once
- **Triton**: a large carved wooden printing block, an object and not an animal, whose face prints a whole square tile of sixteen small rosettes at once, lying beside a sheet printed with several such tiles
- **JAX Pallas**: a flat brass stencil plate, an object and not an animal, with a decorative geometric pattern cut through it, lying on a sheet of paper
- **BLAS microkernel**: a small boxwood abacus
- **Metal**: a steel engraver's die stamp with its sharp relief face
- **OpenCL**: a single brass key lying in front of three differently made padlocks it opens: one brass, one iron, one steel
- **AMD HIP**: a double-ended brass key with a different bit at each end
- **Fused kernel**: an antique folding pocket knife with several blades and tools opened from one handle
- **Operator dispatch**: a ring of keys with one key turned outwards, chosen
- **TensorFlow OpKernel**: an opened plaster mould with the cast seashell it produced lying beside it
- **Compute shader**: a square mosaic panel of identical small square glass tiles in cobalt and gold, an object and not an animal, with a few loose tiles beside it waiting to be set

## IV · The Weighting: a function of two places

- **CNN filter**: the compound eye of a fly seen close: a lattice of small identical lenses
- **Convolution**: a brass-rimmed magnifying glass lying over a printed row of dots of different sizes, the dots under the lens enlarged and softly blurred
- **Image filter**: a square of fine brass sieve mesh in a wooden frame
- **Integral kernel**: a small harp whose strings, of different thicknesses, join its two arms
- **Density estimation**: a row of small bell-shaped harebell flowers of different heights growing side by side
- **Heat kernel**: a single coal ember with shimmering rings of heat spreading outward around it
- **Markov kernel**: a pair of antique ivory dice
- **Probability kernel**: a Galton board: a wooden board of pins with small balls piled into a bell-shaped heap in the slots below
- **Morphology**: a set of brass punches with cross, square and disc-shaped tips
- **Averaging kernel**: a lens of frosted glass in a brass ring
- **Bethe–Salpeter**: two cherries joined on one stem
- **Cauchy kernel**: a closed loop of brass wire encircling a single pearl
- **Collision kernel**: two drops of quicksilver merging into one
- **Density kernel**: a glass bell jar
- **Dirichlet kernel**: a ruffled cockle shell with a tall central crest and rippled edges
- **Seed dispersal**: a dandelion seed head releasing its seeds, which drift away: most stay close to the head, a few travel far
- **Exchange–correlation**: a cluster of soap bubbles, each pressing a hollow into its neighbours
- **Fejér kernel**: a smooth bell-shaped mushroom cap with fine gills beneath
- **Generating function**: a corner of a woven cane lattice, its strands making diagonal paths
- **Green function**: a single drop of water falling into a shallow round dish, rings spreading out and reflecting from the rim
- **Hilbert transform**: a pair of mirror-image spiral seashells, one coiling left and one coiling right
- **Reconstruction**: a brass French curve drafting template
- **Kernel polynomial method**: a steel tuning fork with fading rings of sound around its tines
- **Kernel regression**: a draughtsman's flexible wooden spline, an object and not an animal: a thin wooden batten bent into a smooth curve and held in shape by four heavy grey lead spline weights resting against it
- **Memory kernel**: a brass pendulum bob swinging inside a glass jar of honey
- **Mollifier**: a smooth, rounded river pebble beside the jagged rock fragment it was worn from
- **Poisson kernel**: a round drum with a tensioned skin and a laced rim
- **Radiation point kernel**: a thick lead brick with a small glass vial of radium glowing faintly behind it
- **Sensitivity kernel**: an antique brass seismometer: a heavy pendulum with a fine stylus
- **Singular kernel**: a single needle-sharp sea urchin spine
- **SPH smoothing**: a small heap of clear glass marbles
- **Stein identity**: a brass balance scale resting in perfect equilibrium
- **Fourier / Laplace**: a glass prism splitting a ray of light into a spectrum
- **Volterra series**: three nested glass handbells of graduated sizes

## V · The Similarity: a function of two things

- **SVM kernel**: two scallop shells, one fitted perfectly over the other
- **Gaussian / RBF**: two overlapping circular ripples spreading on still water
- **Gaussian process**: a long string of pearls whose sizes change smoothly from one to the next
- **Neural tangent kernel**: two leaves from the same tree laid side by side, their vein patterns matching
- **Reproducing kernel**: a seashell and its exact reflection in a small hand mirror
- **Linear attention**: a spider's web with threads of different thickness running from the centre to dewdrops
- **Quantum kernel**: two soap bubbles pressed together, their shared wall and iridescent interference colours where they overlap
- **Bergman kernel**: a polished agate slice with smooth, flowing colour bands
- **Determinantal kernel**: a pine cone with its scales evenly spaced
- **Fisher kernel**: two feathers laid side by side, compared vane by vane
- **Graph similarity**: two sprigs of coral with similar branching, side by side
- **Kernel mean embedding**: a brass balance with a small heap of shells on each pan
- **Other ML kernels**: a tray of related seashells: a scallop, a cockle, a cowrie and a whelk
- **Neural-network GP**: a fan of many fine feathers whose tips together trace a smooth bell-shaped outline
- **Stein discrepancy**: a magnetic compass with iron filings tracing field lines around it
- **String kernel**: two strings of coloured glass beads laid side by side, sharing several runs of the same colours
- **Szegő kernel**: a ring of small shells arranged around the rim of a round dish

## VI · The Vanishing: what a map sends to zero

- **Nullspace**: a single poppy pressed perfectly flat between two panes of glass, beside the same poppy fresh and upright: pressing has removed one whole direction, its depth
- **Group homomorphism**: an antique clock dial
- **Ring homomorphism**: a toothed brass wheel with every fourth tooth marked in vermilion
- **Categorical kernel**: a compass rose whose arrows all meet at an empty ring at its centre
- **Congruence kernel**: pairs of identical acorns tied together with thread
- **Differential nullspace**: an antique brass spirit level with its glass vial, the air bubble resting exactly at the centre mark
- **Isogeny kernel**: a glass torus paperweight with a few gold flecks inside
- **Kernel pair**: two identical keys tied together on one ring
- **Module / Lie algebra**: a set of nested empty brass rings, like an armillary sphere without its globe
- **Parity-check code**: a strip of punched paper tape

## VII · The Remnant: what survives the pruning

- **Kernelization**: a small bonsai tree pruned back hard to a compact, dense core of branches, the cut twigs lying beside its pot
- **Lossy kernelization**: an apple core with a little flesh still left on it
- **Belief revision**: a small stone arch held up by its keystone
- **Cooperative game**: a round pie cut into equal, fair slices
- **Digraph kernel**: a few separate limpets spaced apart on a stone
- **Discriminating kernel**: a ship in a bottle
- **Kernel Search**: a prospector's gold pan with a few nuggets left after the gravel has been washed out
- **Perfect kernel**: a single snowflake
- **Section kern**: a cross-section of a stone column with a small diamond marked at its centre
- **Semigroup kernel**: three overlapping soap films sharing one small common lens
- **Squarefree kernel**: a cluster of crystals with exactly one of each kind: quartz, pyrite and amethyst
- **Topological set kernel**: an onion cut in half, showing its nested layers
- **Viability kernel**: a sea star sheltering in a shallow stone basin of seawater
- **Visibility kernel**: a polished star sapphire cabochon whose six-rayed star meets in a small central hexagon; the stone is drawn crisply with no glow and no halo around it

## ? · The Odd One Out: fits no family

- **NASA SPICE**: a stack of punched data cards beside a brass sextant
